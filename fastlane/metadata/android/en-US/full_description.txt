Cedro converts images, PDFs, audio and video and turns speech into text, sending nothing anywhere: everything happens on your phone.

• Images: JPG, PNG, WebP, PDF, compression and text from photos (OCR).
• PDF: to Word, text and images. Word, ODT and RTF documents can be shared.
• Audio and video: MP3, WAV, MP4, using the phone's hardware acceleration.
• Speech to text, including WhatsApp and Telegram voice messages, with whisper.cpp and no online service. The first time, it downloads the model (32 or 60 MB).
• "Share with Cedro" from any app: results are saved in Documents/Cedro.
• A full-screen wheel to pick the format with one finger, plus themes and colours to choose from.

No ads, no account, no tracking. The source code is open (MIT licence).

Requires Android 8 or later and a 64-bit (arm64) processor.
